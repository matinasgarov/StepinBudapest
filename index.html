FAQ

Questions parents ask

When should I contact you?

At least 2–4 weeks before your student's arrival date so we have time to arrange everything properly.

Will someone be with my child when they land?

Yes — we personally meet every student at Budapest Airport, no exceptions.

How do I know my child is safe after arrival?

We provide regular check-ins and are reachable on WhatsApp at any time. Parents can contact us directly.

Do you guarantee accommodation?

We provide thoroughly vetted options and guide the final choice. We never recommend anything we haven't verified.

Can parents contact you directly?

Yes — we communicate with both students and parents throughout the entire process.
